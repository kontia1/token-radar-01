A protocol that already trades is not an early launch, so the board holds only the protocols whose own record carries no token: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. Each one is scored on four things beyond its size. Size is a fixed share of its TVL. Momentum is its 7 day TVL change, clamped at both ends because a collapse is not a discount and a spike is usually an incentive program. Age rewards the book that has run for years without issuing anything. Category and chains say whether that TVL sits in a business with a fee to share, on the chains where buyers are. Nothing here predicts a price: it ranks where a launch would matter most, and every row carries the five step plan that follows from its own numbers.

Why each multiplier is there

base — TVL is the only hard number here: capital already trusting the book. The float a token would put in play is a fraction of it, so the base takes 0.22 of TVL: the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple of the snapshot's own token-bearing protocols, held inside a fixed band so a few outliers cannot set it. Without it the board would rank by TVL alone.

momentum — a book people moved into over seven days is the behaviour a launch monetises later. Clamped at −40% and +80%, weighted 0.5: momentum modifies a book, it is not a book.

age — years with no token is the strongest argument a protocol has: the team kept the option open and the TVL survived a drawdown. Full credit at six years, unknown age scored as one.

category — identical TVL means different things in different businesses: lending 1.40, DEX 1.34, perps 1.30, CDP 1.24, restaking 1.22, yield 1.16, staking 1.10, curator 1.08, RWA 1.05, bridge 1.00, games and NFTs 0.80, meme 0.72, anything unmatched 0.92.

chain — Ethereum, Solana and Base carry the deepest liquidity and the widest buyer set; every further chain is another surface to distribute on, up to a cap.

score — the four multipliers multiplied together, divided by the best reachable product, so 100 is the ceiling any protocol could hit and 43 means the row claims under half of it.
